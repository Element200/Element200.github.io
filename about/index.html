About Sane About me I'm a graduate student at Yale University. I tend to work at the interface of biology and physics, with a strong focus on the physics. (I once had a professor who said that there's biophysics and biophysics, and I consider myself a biophysicist.) In fact, three of my four big research projects, although with biophysicists, involved no biology whatsoever; if anything, I worked very hard to ensure that there was no biology in my samples.
