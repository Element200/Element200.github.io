I'm a first-year PhD student at Yale, having just completed my MSc degree at SFU! I currently work in nonequilibrium statmech-y stuff; my undergraduate work was primarily on self-propelling liquid crystal droplets and my MSc work was on nonequilibrium cooling (the Mpemba effect): both with biophysicists although I never actually studied anything biological per se. (If anything, I worked quite hard to kill all of the bacteria in my experiments.)

I like physics, hiking, playing the guitar, and benne dosa. I dislike geese.

  2. A second edition of my crappy high-school paper on the double pendulum. The second edition contains more detail and perhaps explains a bunch of things considerably better; at least, well enough for a fairly dedicated high-school student to understand. Hey, if you're a high-school student looking to spend three months on an extremely complicated project worth 3% of your grade, maybe give it a shot!
