S. S. Sane

I'm a first-year PhD student at Yale, having just completed my MSc degree at SFU! I currently work in nonequilibrium stat-mech-y stuff; my undergraduate work was primarily on self-propelling liquid crystal droplets and my MSc work was on nonequilibrium cooling (the Mpemba effect): both with biophysicists although I never actually studied anything biological per se. (If anything, I worked quite hard to kill all of the bacteria in my experiments.)

I like physics, hiking, playing the guitar, and benne dosa. I dislike geese. I'm generally ambivalent about plastic giraffes.
